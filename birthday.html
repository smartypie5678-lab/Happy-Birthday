Happy Birthday!

Aaj hawa bhi kuch narmi si laayi hai,
Jaise tumhari muskaan ne duniya sajayi hai।
Janamdin ke is khoobsurat mauke par,
Har khushi ne tujhe gale se lagayi hai।

Tumhari baaton mein ek sukoon sa rehta hai,
Jo dil ko bina kahe hi samjha jaata hai।
Dosti ke is pyaare se rishte mein,
Har ehsaas khud-ba-khud gehra ho jaata hai।

Tumhari zindagi ho roshni se bhari,
Tumhare sapne ho sach ke kareeb।
Tum raho hamesha yun hi khaas,
Happy Birthday!
